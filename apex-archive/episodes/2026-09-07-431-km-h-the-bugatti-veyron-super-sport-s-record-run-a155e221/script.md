## Segment 1 -- title
**Headline:** 431 KM/H: BUGATTI'S RECORD RUN
**Narration:** In July 2010, a modified road car did something almost no road car had ever done: it broke 431 kilometers per hour.
**Caption:** In July 2010, a road car broke 431 km/h.

## Segment 2 -- stat_bars
**Narration:** The original Veyron hit 407 kilometers per hour back in 2005. Five years later, the Super Sport pushed that to 431, with Bugatti test driver Pierre-Henri Raphanel behind the wheel at Volkswagen's Ehra-Lessien test track in Germany.
**Caption:** 407 km/h in 2005. 431 km/h in 2010.

## Segment 3 -- timeline
**Narration:** The record wasn't uncontested. In 2013, Guinness briefly stripped the title because the record car's speed limiter had been disabled beyond the setting sold to customers, then reinstated it days later. The Veyron held the crown until a Koenigsegg Agera RS beat it in November 2017.
**Caption:** 2013: title stripped, then reinstated. 2017: dethroned by Koenigsegg.

## Segment 4 -- stat_bars
**Narration:** Bugatti built roughly 450 Veyrons in total across every variant. Only 48 of them were Super Sports -- the version that actually set the record.
**Caption:** Only 48 Super Sports were ever built.
