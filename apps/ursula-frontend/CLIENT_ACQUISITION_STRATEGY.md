## 3. First 10 Clients Playbook

### 3.1 Week 1-2: Warm Network
- [ ] List 50 people in your network who run businesses or freelance
- [ ] DM 10/day on LinkedIn with a specific offer (not a pitch)
- [ ] Template: "Hey [name], I built a [specific thing]. Would it help with [their specific problem]? Here's a link if you want to try it: [payment link]"
- [ ] Goal: 3 conversations per day, 1 sale per week

### 3.2 Week 3-4: Cold Outreach
- [ ] Find 5 Reddit/Discord communities where your ICP hangs out
- [ ] Answer questions, provide value, mention your service when relevant
- [ ] Post 1 case study or result from early clients
- [ ] Goal: 2 sales per week

### 3.3 Week 5-8: Referral Loop
- [ ] Ask every paying client: "Know anyone else who needs this?"
- [ ] Offer 1 month free for successful referrals
- [ ] Goal: 1 referral per client

### 3.4 Outreach Templates

**LinkedIn DM (Lead Packs)**:
> I noticed you're [doing X]. I put together qualified lead lists for businesses like yours — 10 verified leads for $49. Want me to send a sample of what the leads look like?

**LinkedIn DM (Payment Processing)**:
> Saw you're selling [product/service]. I built a payment system with Stripe + PayPal smart routing that picks the cheapest processor per transaction. Saves most businesses 15-30% on fees. Worth a quick look?

**LinkedIn DM (SiteGrade AI)**:
> I ran a quick audit on [their site] and found a few things that could improve conversions. Want me to send the full report? It's $19 for the detailed version.

## 4. Onboarding (Keep It Simple)

### 4.1 Immediate (Within 1 Hour of Payment)
- [ ] Stripe sends payment confirmation automatically
- [ ] You send a personal welcome message (text, email, or DM)
- [ ] Schedule a 15-min kickoff call (Calendly link)

### 4.2 Kickoff Call (15 min)
1. What's the #1 thing you need this to solve? (2 min)
2. Here's exactly what you're getting and when (5 min)
3. Here's how to reach me if anything breaks (2 min)
4. Any questions? (6 min)

### 4.3 Delivery Timeline by Product
| Product | Delivery |
|---------|----------|
| SiteGrade Report | Within 24 hours |
| Lead Pack (10) | Within 48 hours |
| Lead Pack (25) | Within 5 business days |
| Lead Pack (50) | Within 10 business days |
| HYDI Starter/Pro/Enterprise | Account setup within 24 hours |
| Payment Processing Setup | Integration within 5 business days |
| AutoStack Beta | Access within 24 hours |
| Lead Subscriptions | First batch within 5 business days, then monthly |

### 4.4 Follow-Up Cadence
- **Day 1**: Welcome + kickoff
- **Day 3**: "How's it going?" check-in
- **Day 7**: "Here's a tip to get more value from [product]"
- **Day 14**: "Any feedback? Anything I can improve?"
- **Day 30**: "Ready to upgrade?" or "Know anyone who needs this?"

## 7. What NOT to Do Yet

- Do NOT buy ads until you have 10+ paying clients and know your conversion rate
- Do NOT build a CRM until manual tracking breaks (probably at 30+ clients)
- Do NOT hire until MRR consistently covers the hire + your expenses
- Do NOT build new features until existing clients ask for them
- Do NOT discount — if someone can't afford $29/mo, they're not your client yet
