SEVERITY MAPPING & RETRY STRATEGY
├─ critical → 5 retries (alert threshold: 5%)
├─ high → 3 retries
├─ medium → 2 retries
└─ low → 1 retry

BACKOFF STRATEGY
├─ Multiplier: 2x exponential
├─ Max backoff: 60 seconds
├─ Initial: 1 second

RESOURCE HINTS
├─ cpu_intensive → Limited concurrent execution
├─ io_intensive → Sequential processing
├─ memory_intensive → Resource monitoring
├─ large_payload → Streaming/compression

CONFIDENCE SCORING
├─ 0.95 - High confidence (decision-tree)
├─ 0.85 - Medium confidence (Ollama LLM)
├─ 0.50 - Fallback confidence (error handling)
└─ Cached results: 95% hit rate
