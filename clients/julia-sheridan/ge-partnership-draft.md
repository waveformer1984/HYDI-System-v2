## 1. SERVICES

Contractor will provide live ultrasound demonstration services to support GE Healthcare equipment sales:
- Operate designated GE ultrasound equipment during prospect demonstrations
- Perform imaging on consenting volunteer subjects (pre-arranged by either party)
- Narrate equipment capabilities to prospective buyers in plain language
- Follow GE demonstration protocols as directed by the sales team

Contractor will NOT:

- Represent themselves as a GE employee
