## RECOMMENDED APPROACH: SBA MICROLOAN

Why Microloan fits ClearView:
- Range: $500–$50,000 — our $20k ask fits perfectly
- Administered through nonprofit intermediaries (no traditional bank required)
- More flexible credit requirements than standard SBA loans
- No collateral required for amounts under $25k in many programs
- Interest: 8–13% fixed, typically 6-year term
- Timeline: 30–90 days from application to funding

## HOW TO FIND YOUR LOCAL MICROLOAN INTERMEDIARY

1. Go to **sba.gov/local-assistance**
2. Search: "Microloan Intermediaries" + your state
3. Common intermediaries: ACCION USA, Women's Business Centers (WBC), local CDFIs
4. Apply directly through the intermediary — each has its own process
5. ProtoForge HEIDI tracks all deadlines and follow-up reminders

**Women's Business Centers** are particularly well-suited — they often provide free prep assistance alongside the loan.
