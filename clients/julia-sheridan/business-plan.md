## 2. MARKET ANALYSIS

### 2.1 Target Markets
- **Primary:** Expecting mothers, 24–32 weeks, HHI $50k+, within 50-mile service radius
- **Secondary:** Baby shower/gender reveal hosts, OB/GYN offices needing overflow mobile capacity
- **B2B:** GE Healthcare regional sales teams

### 2.2 Market Size
- ~3.7M U.S. births annually
- Elective ultrasound market: ~$1B (2025), growing 8% CAGR
- Mobile/boutique segment: fastest-growing sub-segment driven by experiential demand

### 2.3 Competitive Landscape
| Competitor | Weakness | ClearView Advantage |
|-----------|----------|--------------------|
| Fixed boutique studios | Location-dependent | Fully mobile, comes to client |
| Non-credentialed operators | Safety concerns, poor image quality | Senior RDMS credentials |
| Hospital-based elective | Cold setting, rigid scheduling | Warm, flexible, personalized |
| Chain franchise operators | Impersonal, high overhead | Boutique, relationship-driven |

## 3. OPERATIONS

### 3.1 Equipment
- **Primary:** GE Voluson E8 or E10 (GE Healthcare financing)
- **Backup:** Portable unit (SonoSite or equivalent)
- **Vehicle:** Branded SUV/cargo van with mobile workstation

### 3.2 Staffing (Launch)
- Julia Sheridan — sole operator and owner
- ProtoForge HYDI — AI-managed business ops

### 3.3 Compliance
- All sessions are elective keepsake only — no diagnostic interpretation
- Client informed consent/waiver signed before every session
- ALARA protocol enforced (max 60-min imaging)
- HIPAA-compliant encrypted image storage via HYDI (Supabase)
- Professional liability: $1M/$3M minimum
