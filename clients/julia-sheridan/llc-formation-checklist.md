**ProtoForge sources quotes. Julia selects.**

- Minimum: $1M per claim / $3M aggregate
- Best carrier for allied health: **HPSO** (hpso.com) — specializes in sonographers
- Others: Proassurance, The Doctors Company
- Estimated annual cost: $1,500–$2,500

### General Liability — Required
- Minimum: $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate
- Bundle with professional liability to save 10–15%
- Estimated annual cost: $600–$900

### Commercial Auto — Required if using personal vehicle for business
- Personal auto policies typically exclude business use
- Add commercial endorsement or separate commercial auto policy
- Estimated: $800–$1,400/year

## STEP 7: COMPLIANCE — ELECTIVE ULTRASOUND

Elective (non-diagnostic) ultrasound is legal in all 50 states but subject to state-level professional practice rules. Key requirements for ClearView:

- **No diagnostic interpretation** — sessions are keepsake only
- **Client informed consent waiver** signed before every session
- **ALARA principle** — minimize exposure time (max 60 min imaging)
- Julia's RDMS credentials support compliance in any state
